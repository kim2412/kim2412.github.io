01Who we are and what this covers

Bridgelab (브릿지랩, Korean business registration no. 729-71-00630, “we”) is a one-person studio. This policy covers the data processed through the Threads API by LaunchPilot (Meta app name “Bridgelab Poster”), an internal tool we built and use ourselves, through the Threads API and through YouTube API Services, and to this website.

LaunchPilot is not offered to the public. It is connected to exactly one Threads account, our own @bridgelab.app, and exactly one YouTube channel, our own @bridgelab_app. No other person can sign in or connect an account.

02Data we process via the Threads API

Our own accountThe user ID and username of @bridgelab.app, the posts we publish and their metrics (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares), and our publishing quota.
Replies to our postsReply ID, reply text, author username, timestamp, the post or reply it responds to, and hide status — plus the answer we write.
Keyword search resultsFor public posts found with keywords about AI and developer tools (e.g. “Claude Code”, “vibe coding”, “MCP”): post ID, text, author username, permalink, timestamp, media type, and whether it is a reply or quote — plus a record of any comment or repost we made on it.

We do not collect images or video files, follower lists, direct messages, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.

03Why we use it

  • To publish posts on our own account and see how they perform.
  • To answer replies on our own posts. Routine, low-risk replies (thanks, questions answerable from the post itself) are answered automatically, at most 10 per day; anything sensitive (complaints, pricing, our apps, personal topics, “are you a bot?”) waits in a review queue and is only sent after the owner approves or edits it. Spam gets no answer.
  • To find public posts about AI and developer tools so the owner can read them, leave helpful comments (at most 8 per day, at most once per author every 3 days), repost excellent posts (at most 3 per day, at least 2 hours apart), and build a daily reading list with links.

We never like or follow automatically. We never use this data for advertising, profiling, eligibility decisions, or sale.

04AI writing services (processors, international transfer)

To draft comments and replies and to screen out ads and sensitive posts, we send only the text and author username of the relevant post or reply to one AI text-generation service: Google (Gemini API), Groq, Cloudflare (Workers AI), OpenRouter, Anthropic (Claude), or a model running locally on our own computer. These services may be located outside Korea (e.g. the United States) and receive the text only to process that request. We never send access tokens or any other personal data.

We do not otherwise share, sell, or transfer data to anyone.

05Storage and retention

  • Where: a local SQLite database on the owner's computer. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud database.
  • Keyword search results and data about other people's posts and replies are deleted 30 days after collection.
  • Our own posts and their metrics are kept while we operate the account.
  • If we stop using LaunchPilot, all remaining data is deleted.

06Deletion requests and your rights

Anyone may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of data about them. Email eotmd1212@gmail.com with your Threads username (and, if possible, links to the posts). We act within 10 days and confirm by email. Details: Data deletion instructions.

If you delete a post or reply on Threads, it will not be picked up again, and any stored copy is deleted within 30 days.

07Security

  • The Threads access token is stored only in a local configuration file on the owner's computer and is never shared.
  • The management dashboard only runs on the owner's computer (127.0.0.1).
  • We store the minimum fields needed.

08YouTube API Services (our channel @bridgelab_app)

LaunchPilot uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload videos that Bridgelab made itself to Bridgelab's own YouTube channel, @bridgelab_app. Information processed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy at http://www.google.com/policies/privacy, and use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Scopes requestedyoutube.upload (upload videos and thumbnails to our channel) and youtube.readonly (confirm that the connected channel is @bridgelab_app, and read its subscriber count once a day).
Data we access and storeOur own channel's ID, title, handle and subscriber count; the video ID, URL and privacy status YouTube returns for the videos we upload; and the OAuth refresh token that keeps the connection.
Data we do not accessWe never read or store information about other YouTube users, viewers or channels: no comments, watch history, email addresses or similar.
How we use itOnly to upload our own videos, confirm the upload, and record our channel's growth. We never sell it, use it for advertising, share it with third parties, or send it to AI services.
Storage and deletionOn the owner's computer only (the token in a local configuration file, the rest in local files and a local database). The subscriber count is refreshed daily with an authorized API call. If access is revoked or we stop using LaunchPilot, stored YouTube API data and the token are deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access: you can revoke LaunchPilot's (“BridgeLab Uploader”) access to your data at any time via the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Cookies and device information: LaunchPilot does not place cookies on, or collect information from, anyone's device. This website sets no cookies: it only remembers the language you pick in your own browser's local storage, and it loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you tap an app link, our server records the time, the link, the channel you came from, your country and a device class (phone/desktop) — no cookies and no IP address.
